disabled champion with identical seeds and both candidate seats. The screen
used two distinct public archetypes (COK V7 and lonespear V17); the sealed
confirm reserved lonespear V18 and V23 with disjoint opponent, episode, seed,
and time identities.

was invariant to injected episode, submission, and seed metadata. In the live
four-match screen, however, the precursor did not fire. Candidate and champion
therefore tied exactly for rank, reward, margin, lower-tail, and worst metrics.
The runtime ratio was 0.9800, and invalid actions and contract violations were
zero.

The strict screen gate rejected the axis for no primary KPI improvement and no
live economic firing. As required, the untouched confirm panel was not run.
`SEQUENCE_PRECURSOR_POLICY` remains default `False`; the submission contract
passes, no archive was regenerated, and no Kaggle submission was performed.
